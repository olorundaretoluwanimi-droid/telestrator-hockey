# THE SLOT — "Bookends" (Sports Telestrator sample, ≈2:45)

Unofficial fan analysis of **UMass Boston 8, Plymouth State 5** — NCAA Division III men's ice hockey, Little East Conference, Friday 6 February 2026, Edward T. Barry Ice Rink, Boston. Footage: UMass Boston Athletics' own highlight video, released on YouTube under CC BY. The show *THE SLOT* is fictional.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Eight goals, chronological** — every UMass Boston goal in order, each with one freeze. Clear, but eight equal beats is a highlight reel with chalk on it; nothing builds.
2. **One goal from every angle** — only the 7.7-second power-play goal, taken apart live / rewind / three freezes / replay / board. Deep, but at 2–3 minutes it runs out of findings and loses the 4–4 swing that makes the night interesting.
3. **Bookends (chosen)** — the night opens with a goal **ten seconds** after the first faceoff and UMass Boston's scoring closes with one **7.7 seconds** before the second intermission. The opener gets the stopwatch over live play; the middle goals get light treatments (freeze-in-play, dual-angle freeze, a board for the Plymouth State comeback that isn't in the footage); the last goal gets the full grammar. A shot map flattens the last strike frame and drops every goal onto one rink. Structure = two clocks: one counting up from the opening drop, one counting down to the buzzer.

Why 3: it has a turn (4–1 becomes 4–4 in the second period), a build (light → deep), and an ending that echoes the opening (stopwatch up → stopwatch down).

## Logline and arc

Ten seconds in, UMass Boston scored; 7.7 seconds before the intermission, they scored again — what happened in between is a 4–1 lead thrown away and taken back.
- **Setup**: sting, match record card, goal 1 with a running stopwatch (0:10).
- **Turn**: 4–1 after three more goals… board: Plymouth State score three, two of them 15 seconds apart — 4–4.
- **Answer**: Krivtsov, Teply, Kurtas on the power play (light treatments).
- **Ending**: Mortson's goal with 7.7 s left — deep breakdown; flatten into the shot map; data card; end card.

## Benchmark

Studio telestrator segments in televised hockey analysis (intermission panels drawing on paused game video), and explainer channels that annotate real game film.
- **Learn**: one finding per freeze, the replay as a second pass one layer deeper, puck path drawn as the hero line, the habit of naming the space ("the slot", "the backdoor") rather than the player.
- **Don't take**: any broadcaster's logo, fonts, colours, idents, show names or segment names; no network-style scorebug copy.

## Cue map

About 120 BPM sampled band (drum kit, electric bass, staccato brass, timpani, glockenspiel). Groove restarts at each played segment's head; **every freeze stops the drums**: brass stab + timpani + thump + shutter, then hi-hat only. Goals: big brass chord + crash + glockenspiel on each score flip. Board pages: half-time groove. Flatten: timpani roll → stab when the board settles. End: held chord, two glock strokes.
Two near-silences: before the turn board (7) and at freeze ③ of the deep breakdown.
